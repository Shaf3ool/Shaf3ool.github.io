01 — Flagship product

A complete stock and point-of-sale system for Algerian shops, designed and built from scratch. It runs fully offline on a phone or a PC, speaks three languages, and is licensed per machine in two editions: Basic and Pro.

  • ~8,600lines of C++17 core, 35 files
  • 0compiler warnings, automated tests
  • 3languages: Arabic (RTL), French, English
  • 100%offline, no internet needed

  • Sell fast

    A touch till on PC with barcode-scanner support and keyboard shortcuts; receipts for 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers.

  • Stock that fits a real shop

    Sizes × colours inventory, barcode labels, purchase orders and suppliers, all with product photos.

  • Customers & credit

    Supplier and customer credit, loyalty points, VAT invoices and profit-and-loss reports.

  • One file, any device

    An offline single-file app for phone and PC. Nothing to install; backup and restore built in.

  • Shop hub (Pro)

    A C++ LAN hub lets several phones and PCs share one stock over the shop Wi-Fi, with per-device isolation, an admin view and a customer display.

  • Licensed per machine

    Offline activation with Ed25519-signed keys tied to the machine ID and shop name, plus a key generator for Basic and Pro editions.

Basic

One device. The full app for a single shop counter, 14-day free trial with everything open.

Pro

Several devices through the shop hub. Same file, upgraded by key: nothing to reinstall, data stays.

Also delivered: user guides in English and Arabic, a hub setup guide and a seller guide. Being commercialised in Algeria.
